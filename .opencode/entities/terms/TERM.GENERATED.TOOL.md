**Generated Tool** — an `.opencode/tools/` file produced by a scaffolding process rather than hand-written. Its structure is deterministic, its metadata annotations (`// @toolclass`) are schema-derived, and its compliance is automatically verifiable against the six constraints of PROT.TOOL.GENERATION.COMPLIANCE. Differs from hand-written tools in that its entire surface is determined by the project's manifests — changes to manifests regenerate the tool rather than requiring manual edits.
