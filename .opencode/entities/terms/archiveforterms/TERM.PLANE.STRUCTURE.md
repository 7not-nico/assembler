**Structure Plane — Project Classification** — distinguishes directories that hold multiple sub-projects from those that are single units. Container dirs (e.g. `code-dives/`) group independent sub-projects under a common parent; leaf dirs are standalone.

**leaf** — single project or non-project directory. No sub-projects at immediate level.

**container** — holds multiple independent sub-projects, each potentially with its own AGENTS.md and tooling. The container itself is not a project.
