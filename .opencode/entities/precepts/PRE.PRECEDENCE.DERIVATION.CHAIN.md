**Precedence Derivation** — meaning through what precedes.

## Corollaries

- Every entity defaults to Ring 4 (term), meaning empty, until derived
- Derivation proceeds outer→inner: ask "what precedes this entity?"
- `precedes` points to inner declared entities — only actual patlib entities, not hypothetical variants or infinite succession
- Dead ends in derivation = cognitions (Ring 1) — no further inner ring exists
- Leaf become terms only when no further derivation is warranted by reasoning about what IT precedes
- Rings describe the entity's nature — the layer where it sits after derivation
- If `precedes` traversal forms a cycle (traversing returns to a visited entity), the entities form a domain
- A domain requires an encompassing term that labels and brackets the cycle
- The encompassing term is derived from the concept that grounds the domain
- Cycle members share the same source; the encompassing term's `precedes` points to the cycle start

## Applicability

All entity creation and classification. Applied at declaration time: start with an unmeaning term, trace the precedence chain, derive the entity's ring and meaning.
