**Erich Fromm** — German-American social psychologist, psychoanalyst, and humanistic philosopher. Core contributor to the Frankfurt School's critical theory before departing over theoretical differences with Horkheimer and Adorno. Developed **social character** theory bridging Marxian social analysis with Freudian psychoanalysis. Major works develop the freedom-as-burden thesis and authoritarianism's psychological roots; humanistic ethics; critique of alienated social structures; love as acquired skill, not passive emotion; biophilia–necrophilia dichotomy; and the having–being distinction in modes of existence. Political activist co-founding SANE (Committee for a Sane Nuclear Policy) and Amnesty International. Taught at Columbia, Bennington, UNAM, Michigan State, and NYU.
