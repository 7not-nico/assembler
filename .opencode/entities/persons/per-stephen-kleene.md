**Stephen Cole Kleene** (1909–1994) — American mathematician and logician. Doctoral student of Alonzo Church at Princeton (Ph.D. 1934). Co-discovered the Kleene–Rosser paradox (1935), proving Church's original logical system inconsistent. Founded recursion theory, proving the equivalence of λ-definability with Herbrand–Gödel recursiveness (1936). Developed the Kleene star (Kleene closure), Kleene's recursion theorem / fixed-point theorem, and the Kleene normal form theorem. The Kleene hierarchy (arithmetical hierarchy) and Kleene's T predicate are fundamental in computability theory. Author of *Introduction to Metamathematics* (1952), a standard text in mathematical logic.
