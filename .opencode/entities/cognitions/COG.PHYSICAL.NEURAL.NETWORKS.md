**Physical Neural Networks (PNNs)** — neural networks where computation is performed directly by physical processes on controllable substrates, trained via physics-aware training (PAT), a hybrid in situ–in silico backpropagation algorithm. Demonstrated substrates include optical (second-harmonic generation), mechanical (multimode plate oscillations), and electronic (nonlinear oscillator circuits). PNNs divide into isomorphic variants (memristor crossbars mimicking digital operations) and broken-isomorphism variants (exploiting native physical dynamics without mathematical equivalence). Time-multiplexed architectures (e.g., TIDAL-Net) extend effective depth while reusing physical hardware.
