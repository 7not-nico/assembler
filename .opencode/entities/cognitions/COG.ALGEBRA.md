**Algebra** — The mathematical study of abstract structures and the operations that act on them. From Arabic *al-jabr* "reunion of broken parts" (al-Khwarizmi, 820 CE). Algebra studies the logic of combining elements under operations: groups (symmetry of composition), rings (addition + multiplication), fields (division permitted), vector spaces (scaling + addition). It is the language of structure itself — any system where elements combine under rules is an algebraic system.

Algebra grounds Boolean algebra (two-valued logic as a ring), linear algebra (vector spaces and matrices), and number theory (the integers as a ring). It is the outermost mathematical cognition for any system that reasons about structure and transformation.
