**Proof** — the foundational cognition that a statement follows necessarily from accepted premises through valid reasoning. In the Encyclopedic topology, "proof" is a cognition dead end: the intuitive notion of establishing truth by deduction is so fundamental that no further inner entity precedes it. Proof as a cognition underlies formal proof systems (CON.MATH.FORMALPROOF), interactive proofs (TERM.CRYPTO.INTERACTIVEPROOF), and zero-knowledge proofs (TERM.CRYPTO.ZEROKNOWLEDGEPROOF).
