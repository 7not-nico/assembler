**Hebbian Physics Networks (HPNs)** — a self-organizing computational framework where transport geometry adapts via residual-driven anti-Hebbian updates in response to local conservation violations. Residuals (imbalances in continuity, momentum, or energy) act as thermodynamic forces driving joint evolution of state and constitutive weights. Near equilibrium, the operator converges to symmetric positive-definite form reproducing Onsager's reciprocal relations. Far from equilibrium, the system self-organizes into transport topologies without global loss functions or backpropagated objectives. Grounded in Prigogine's dissipative structures.
