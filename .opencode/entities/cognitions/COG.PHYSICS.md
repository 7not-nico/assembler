**Physics** — The empirical science of matter, energy, space, and time. From Greek *physis* "nature" — the study of the natural world through observation, experiment, and mathematical model. Physics seeks the fundamental laws governing the behavior of the universe, from subatomic particles to galactic clusters.

Physics is a cognition dead end — no outer ring precedes it. It is the empirical foundation that grounds all physical sciences. Its major branches include classical mechanics (motion and forces), electromagnetism (electric and magnetic fields), thermodynamics (heat and energy), quantum mechanics (discrete states), and relativity (space-time). Physics provides the laws that chemistry, materials science, and engineering discover and apply.
