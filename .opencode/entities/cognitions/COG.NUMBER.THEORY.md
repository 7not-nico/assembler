**Number Theory** — The mathematical study of numbers and their properties. From Greek *arithmos* "number" + *theoria* "contemplation." Number theory investigates the structure and behavior of integers: divisibility, primality, Diophantine equations, modular arithmetic, and the distribution of primes. It is the oldest branch of pure mathematics — Euclid's *Elements* (300 BCE) contains foundational results about primes and factorization.

Number theory grounds the concept of number itself — what it means to count, to represent quantity in a positional system, and to encode information as digits. Binary representation (base-2) is a number-theoretic construction: every integer has a unique representation as a sum of powers of two. This is the mathematical fact that makes machine code possible — any instruction or address can be encoded as a sequence of bits.
