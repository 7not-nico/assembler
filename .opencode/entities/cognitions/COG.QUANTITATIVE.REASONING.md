**Quantitative Reasoning** — analysis of a situation into a quantitative structure, involving identification of measurable variables, modeling relationships between them, and applying mathematical reasoning to interpret, predict, and decide. Related to COG.MATH (mathematics as domain) and COG.LOGICAL.OPERATORS (logical reasoning foundations). NEX.META.DECISION.CANVAS provides a structured approach for applying QR to architectural decisions.
