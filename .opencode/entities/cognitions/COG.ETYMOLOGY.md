**Etymology** — the study of the origin of words and the way their meanings have changed throughout history. From Greek *etymon* "true sense" (neut. of *etymos* "true, real, actual") + *logos* "word, study." Etymology traces words back through time to their roots in older languages (Latin, Greek, Proto-Indo-European) to uncover the concepts embedded in them.

Etymology is a cognition because it is a domain of knowing — a systematic body of knowledge about how words carry history within them. It is not invented: words have real etymologies that can be discovered through comparative and historical methods. The domain encompasses sound change laws (Grimm's Law, Verner's Law), cognate identification, root reconstruction, and semantic drift patterns.
