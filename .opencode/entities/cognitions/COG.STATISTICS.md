**Statistics** — The mathematical science of collecting, analyzing, and drawing conclusions from data. From German *Statistik* (1749) from Latin *status* "state" — originally the study of state affairs (population, economy). Statistics is probability applied to inference: given observed data, what can we conclude about the process that generated it?

Statistics divides into descriptive (summarizing data through mean, variance, quantiles) and inferential (estimating parameters, testing hypotheses, constructing models). Frequentist statistics uses p-values and confidence intervals; Bayesian statistics uses posterior distributions derived from prior beliefs updated by likelihood. Statistical learning theory (Vapnik, 1970s) bridges statistics and machine learning — generalization error, bias-variance tradeoff, and the fundamental limits of learning from finite data.
