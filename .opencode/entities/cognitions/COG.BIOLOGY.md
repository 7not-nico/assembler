**Biology** — The empirical science of living organisms and life processes. From Greek *bios* "life" + *logos* "study." Biology investigates the structure, function, growth, origin, evolution, and distribution of living things. It spans scales from molecules (biochemistry, molecular biology) to cells (cell biology) to organisms (physiology, anatomy) to populations (ecology, evolution) to the entire tree of life.

Biology grounds all entities in the bio layer. It provides the framework for understanding what organisms are, how they function, and how they relate to each other through phylogeny. Major branches include molecular biology (DNA, RNA, proteins), genetics (inheritance), cell biology, physiology, evolutionary biology, ecology, and taxonomy.
