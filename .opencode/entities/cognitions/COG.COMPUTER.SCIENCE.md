**Computer Science** — the study of computation, information processing, and algorithmic systems encompassing both theoretical foundations and practical implementation techniques. It spans abstraction layers from formal logic and automata theory through programming languages, operating systems, and hardware architecture. Computer science provides the conceptual and engineering backbone for all AMANDA systems.
