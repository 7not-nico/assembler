**Physics-Defined Neural Networks** — three paradigms embedding physical laws into neural computation at fundamentally different depths: substrate identity (Physical Neural Networks, where physical systems perform computation directly), loss structure (Physics-Informed Neural Networks, where PDE constraints regularize digital NNs), and dynamical laws (Hebbian Physics Networks, where local conservation-driven adaptation replaces global loss functions).
