**Chemistry** — The empirical science of atoms, molecules, and their interactions. From Greek *khemia* "transmutation" — the study of how elements combine, separate, and transform through chemical reactions. Chemistry investigates the properties of elements (organized in the periodic table by atomic number), the formation of chemical bonds (covalent, ionic, metallic), and the energy changes that accompany reactions.

Chemistry grounds the behavior of materials at the atomic and molecular level. The classical computer depends on the chemistry of silicon (element 14): silicon's electron configuration (1s²2s²2p⁶3s²3p²) determines its semiconducting properties. Doping — introducing impurity atoms (phosphorus, boron) — alters silicon's conductivity through controlled chemical substitution. Without chemistry, there are no transistors.
