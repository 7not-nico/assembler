**Calculus** — The mathematical study of continuous change. From Latin *calculus* "small stone used for counting." Calculus formalizes two fundamental operations: differentiation (rates of change, slopes) and integration (accumulation, areas under curves). Developed independently by Newton (fluxions, 1660s) and Leibniz (calculus, 1684).

Calculus grounds optimization — finding the minimum or maximum of a function by following its gradient. Every neural network learns through gradient-based optimization (backpropagation). The classical computer approximates continuous calculus through discrete operations: finite differences, numerical integration, iterative convergence. Without calculus, there is no learning in the modern sense — only static computation.
