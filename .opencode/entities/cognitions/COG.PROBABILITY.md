**Probability** — The mathematical study of uncertainty. From Latin *probabilitas* "provability, credibility." Probability formalizes the likelihood of events under uncertainty through a measure space (sample space, event sigma-algebra, probability measure). Kolmogorov (1933) established the axiomatic foundation: non-negativity, normalization (total probability = 1), and countable additivity.

Probability grounds all reasoning under uncertainty. Three major interpretations exist: frequentist (long-run frequency of repeated trials), Bayesian (degree of belief updated by evidence), and algorithmic (Kolmogorov complexity). Probability is the mathematics of information — Shannon's information theory derives entropy directly from the probability distribution of a random variable.
