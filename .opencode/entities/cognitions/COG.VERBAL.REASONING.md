**VerbalReasoning** — capacity to comprehend, analyze, and evaluate written material through identifying main ideas, drawing inferences, recognizing author assumptions, understanding structural relationships, and reasoning from textual evidence. Related to COG.QUANTITATIVE.REASONING (parallel reasoning term) and COG.LOGICAL.OPERATORS (logical foundations for argument analysis). NEX.META.DECISION.CANVAS applies when verbal reasoning informs architectural decisions.
