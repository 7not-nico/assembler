**Code Composition Ring** — builds compose ring by ring from the world of ideas outward, in three groups. Each step corresponds to one ring of `RING.ELEMENT.PRECEDENCE`; a step composes only inner steps; no step skips a ring. The world of ideas forms the base — the pure conceptual realm exists before any name or reality composes onto it.

## Step-by-step

### Group I — Formation (r0–r5)

The formation procedure builds the substrate, gives it reality, and opens it to meaning.

- **Hold the world of ideas (r0)** — list the concepts of the build; the pure conceptual realm. Bind nothing, name nothing, count nothing.
- **Shape the abstracts (r1)** — give each idea an abstract shape; non-action nouns. Representation only — no numbers, no directions.
- **Bind (r2)** — a name to each abstract shape. The name attaches to the object. Uses propositions
- **Structure (r3)** — hold the reality, give persistence. The structure survives the build. Action Verbs
- **Compose logical gates (r4)** — operations over the structure. New meaning arises; life expands and contradicts.
- **Compose multitudinal representation (r5)** — many manners of evaluating expressions. Reality twists to accommodate the mandates.

### Group II — Logical Gates (r1, r2, r3)

The gating procedure inverts and combines signals.

- **Negate (r1)** — invert a signal; the gate of contradiction.
- **Conjoin (r2)** — require both inputs; the gate of expansion.
- **Disjoin (r3)** — accept either input; the gate of choice.

### Group III — Composition (r6–r10)

The composition procedure composes types and units onto the formed substrate.

- **Compose a named abstract (r6)** — class: methods + attributes into one type.
- **Compose the units (r7)** — module: classes, abstracts, gates into one file.
- **Compose the modules (r8)** — package: modules into one tree.
- **Wrap (r9)** — decorator: wrap a function or class.
- **Shape the classes (r10)** — metaclass: shape the types; the terminus.

## Layer discipline

- One step per ring; a step composes only what inner steps supply
- A step verifies before the next composes — hold, shape, bind, structure, gate, compose, import in order
- No step skips — Group I forms r0 → r5, Group II gates r1/r2/r4, Group III composes r6 → r10
- A build stops at the outermost ring its task requires

## Applicability

Program construction in the workspace: any derivation-driven build. Composes with `RING.ELEMENT.PRECEDENCE` (element ordering) and `RING.LANGUAGE.TOPOLOGY` (language escalation).
