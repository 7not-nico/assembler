# India — Verbal Reasoning / Verbal Ability

## Rating: WARN (commercial-heavy ecosystem)

## Note on Source Quality
India's verbal reasoning landscape is dominated by commercial exam-prep publishers (Amazon, Flipkart, BodheePrep, SelfStudys, Scribd) and coaching materials. Few .ac.in academic sources exist for "verbal ability" as a research field. This reflects that verbal reasoning in India is primarily framed as a competitive exam skill (CAT, GMAT, Bank, SSC) rather than an academic discipline. Key exam-prep texts noted for reference but academic replacement was limited.

## Key Findings
- India uses "Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension" (VARC) as the dominant term, not "Verbal Reasoning"
- CAT exam VARC section comprises: Reading Comprehension passages (~16 questions) + Verbal Ability (~8 questions: parajumbles, odd sentences, summary)
- Verbal Ability includes: parajumbles (reorder sentences), odd sentence identification, paragraph summary, paragraph completion
- Grammar and vocabulary no longer tested in CAT but remain in other Indian management exams
- Ecosystem is heavily commercial with limited academic research
