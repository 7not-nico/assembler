## Key Findings
- PISA reading literacy is the dominant framework for assessing reading comprehension internationally
- Finland scores above OECD average (490 vs 476) but experienced significant decline from peak performance
- Gender gap in reading favoring girls is substantial (45 points in Finland)
- Critical literacy research at University of Jyväskylä analyzes how PISA operationalizes critical reading
- PISA defines reading as understanding, using, and reflecting on written texts
