## Key Findings
- East Asian research focuses on L2 (English) reading comprehension rather than native-language verbal reasoning
- Nagoya University research shows L1 literacy is a strong predictor of L2 reading comprehension
- J-STAGE research shows AI tools for generating reading comprehension questions are viewed as useful by English teachers in Japan
- Korea University research explores reading-writing connection for EFL learners
- Limited research on "verbal reasoning" as a construct — framed as reading comprehension and language proficiency
