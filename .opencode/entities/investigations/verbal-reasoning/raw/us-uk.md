## Key Findings
- Verbal Reasoning as measured by GRE comprises three core skills: reading comprehension, text completion, sentence equivalence
- Reading comprehension involves analysis, inference, summarization, structure awareness, author perspective identification
- Text Completion assesses ability to reason from partial information to construct coherent meaning
- Sentence Equivalence focuses on understanding meaning of completed wholes
- Reading Rockets provides comprehensive research synthesis on comprehension instruction strategies
