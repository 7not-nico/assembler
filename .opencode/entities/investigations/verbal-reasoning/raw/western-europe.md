## Key Findings
- Reading comprehension theory is well-established: Kintsch construction-integration model (surface → textbase → situation model) is the dominant framework
- Simple View of Reading: comprehension = decoding × language comprehension
- PIRLS framework defines four comprehension processes used in international assessment
- Reading Systems Framework (Perfetti & Stafura): word identification + word-to-text integration
- Comprehension strategies divide into cognitive (direct) and metacognitive (monitoring)
