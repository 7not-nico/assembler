# Region: Greek — PRINCIPLE (ἀρχή)

## Sources
- Perseus/LSJ: *archē* (ἀρχή) — beginning, origin, first principle, sovereignty
- SEP (Presocratics): Thales — water as *archē*; Anaximander — *to apeiron* as *archē*
- Perseus (Aristotle Metaphysics 1013a): six senses of *archē* — starting point of motion, knowledge, etc.
- UChicago (Pre-Socratic): *archē* as ultimate principle of unity underlying diversity
- Academia (Progettare): semantic development of *archē* in Pre-Socratic philosophy

## Key Findings
- **archē** from *archō* "to begin, rule" → dual meaning: (1) temporal beginning, (2) sovereign power
- Anaximander (6th C BC) first to use *archē* as technical term for "first principle"
- Aristotle *Metaphysics* 1013a: six definitions — beginning of motion, best starting point, immanent origin, external source, deliberate choice, knowledge starting point
- Latin *principium* = direct translation of Greek *archē*
- Key phrase: *archē kai telos* — "beginning and end"
