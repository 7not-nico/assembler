# Region: German — PRINCIPLE (Prinzip, Grundsatz)

## Sources
- Kant (Grundlegung): *Prinzip* used interchangeably with *Grundsatz*; transcendental vs practical principles
- Kant (KdU): transcendentale Prinzipien vs metaphysische Prinzipien
- Pollok (USC): Kant's hypothetical vs categorical imperatives — principles of will
- Princeton: *Begriff* (concept) vs *begreifen* (grasp) — Kant's distinction; *Grundsatz* as fundamental principle
- Academia (Heuristisch legitimierte Grundsätze): Kant's principles of homogeneity, specification, continuity

## Key Findings
- Kant uses *Prinzip* and *Grundsatz* interchangeably (translator's note)
- *Grundsatz* = "ground-sentence" — foundational proposition
- Kant's transcendental principles: a priori conditions of possible experience
- Distinction: *Prinzip* (objective, universal) vs *Maxime* (subjective, agent-relative)
- Three kinds: theoretical principles (nature), practical principles (freedom), aesthetic principles (judgment)
