# Region: Latin — PRINCIPLE (principium)

## Sources
- Perseus/L&S: *principium* — beginning, origin; from *princeps* (*primus* + *capio*)
- Perseus/L&S: *princeps* — "first-taker", chief, originator, emperor
- Perseus: *primus* — first; PIE *per- "forward"
- SEP (Aristotle Metaphysics): *archai* as first principles; PNC
- Bentham (UT Austin): principle = *primus* + *capio* "to take"
- MEC (Michigan): principle from OF *principe* & L *principium*
- Dickinson: *principium* = beginning, origin; *princeps* = "first citizen"

## Key Findings
- **principium** = *princeps* (*primus* "first" + *capio* "take") → "that which takes first"
- Cicero: *principia rerum* "first principles of things"; *principia naturalia* "natural principles"
- Bentham (IPML 1789): explicit decomposition of principle into *primus* + *capio*
- "Petitio principii" = "begging the question" — assuming the starting point
- In medieval logic: *principium* translated Aristotle's *archē*
