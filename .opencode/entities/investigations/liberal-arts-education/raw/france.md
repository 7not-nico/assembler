## Key Findings (France)

1. **Pluridisciplinarity**: French humanities license deliberately spans 4 disciplines (letters, history, philosophy, languages); students attend courses alongside specialists
2. **Competency framework**: Shift from knowledge-based to competency-based training; transversal skills (autonomy, analysis, synthesis, initiative, relational) explicitly defined
3. **Employability focus**: Professional insertion central; mandatory internships; pre-professionalization tracks; humanities graduates valued for critical thinking, synthesis, writing
4. **Critical thinking**: Formation of critical mind (esprit critique) as core mission; loving knowledge, learning to learn for life
5. **International dimension**: Strong foreign language requirement; 90-100% study abroad at top programs (Strasbourg)

## Gaps
- Less empirical research on humanities outcomes vs. US/Nordic countries
- Tension between national diploma standardization and local initiative in competency frameworks
- Limited intake (elite) — not universal model
