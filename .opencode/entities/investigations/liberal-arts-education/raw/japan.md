## Key Findings (Japan)

1. **Critical thinking (批判的思考)**: Central outcome — defined as evidence-based, logical, unbiased thinking; taught via debate, discussion, argument analysis (Kyoto, Yamaguchi)
2. **Employability**: Liberal arts + data science emphasized; employers value autonomy, flexibility, communication, broad perspective (Teikyo survey)
3. **Multi-perspective thinking**: 多角的視点 (multi-angle perspective) and 俯瞰 (bird's-eye) problem-solving consistently cited across all Japanese universities
4. **Ethical formation**: Ethical perspective (倫理観) highest-rated outcome (TWCU survey); social justice, diversity, gender education emphasized
5. **International dimension**: Study abroad integrated; English-medium liberal arts programs (iCLA); foreign language proficiency valued

## Gaps
- Less systematic causal research vs. US; more case-study and survey-based
- Liberal arts often a supplement within specialized Japanese university structure, not a standalone institutional model
- Employer survey response rate low (4%), limits generalizability
