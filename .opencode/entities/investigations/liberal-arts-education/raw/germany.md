## Key Findings (Germany)

1. **Bildung tradition**: Humboldtian ideal — education forms mature, critical, autonomous citizens; unity of Bildung (formation) and Ausbildung (training)
2. **Key competencies**: Interdisciplinary thinking, critical thinking, reflection ability, general education, discourse competence, lifelong learning, social responsibility (SG Netzwerk Nord)
3. **Employability**: Key qualifications (Schlüsselqualifikationen) explicitly valued; humanities graduates valued for structured thinking, argumentation, rapid adaptation to new fields (Uni Würzburg)
4. **Historical mission**: Post-WWII re-founding of SG as response to elite failure — civic and political education core purpose (Mainz, Köln)
5. **Structure**: Modularized with professional orientation (Berufsorientierung) and general studies; practical skills + foreign languages + IT

## Gaps
- SG is typically a supplement to major, not a whole-curriculum approach — different from US liberal arts colleges
- Empirical outcomes research on SG less developed than US counterparts
