## Key Findings (English-speaking)

1. **Employer demand**: 90%+ employers value liberal education outcomes; critical thinking, communication, teamwork consistently top-ranked (AAC&U 2006–2021)
2. **Civic formation**: Liberal arts curriculum → democratic engagement through classroom discussion, exposure to diverse perspectives, service learning (Campbell et al.)
3. **Noncognitive development**: Liberal arts develops self-awareness, empathy, open-mindedness, pro-social values, agency (UChicago Consortium)
4. **Cognitive gains**: Indirect cognitive benefits via instructional clarity and deep-learning environments in liberal arts colleges; interactional diversity → critical thinking (Wabash National Study)
5. **Balance breadth & depth**: Employers value well-rounded education + technical skills equally

## Gaps
- Few longitudinal studies isolating liberal arts vs. other college types for non-US contexts
- Causal mechanisms still under investigation (self-selection vs. treatment effect)
