# Round 2 — National Geographic

**Status:** PASS

## Editorial model

- **Pre-2015:** Nonprofit NatGeo Society. Staff-written, rigorous, photo-first
- **2015:** 73% sold to 21st Century Fox ($725M joint venture)
- **2019:** Disney inherits Fox's 73% stake
- **June 2023:** All 19 staff writers laid off, work outsourced to freelancers
- Newsstand sales ended. Audio department eliminated
- 6 top editors laid off in Sep 2022 reorganization

## History

- Founded 1888 by National Geographic Society (133 founding members including A.G. Bell)
- Pioneer of photojournalism and exploration sponsorship
- Gilbert Grosvenor transformed from academic journal to mass-market icon (1899)
- Yellow border since 1910

## Subject scope

- Geography, archaeology, natural science, world history, culture, current events, photography
- Broadest scope of all four publications

## Business model

- For-profit (since 2015). Disney owns 73%, NatGeo Society 27%
- Ad-supported + subscriptions
- 40 local-language editions globally
- Print circ: 1.8M (down from 12M US-only peak in 1980s)
- ~60M readers globally

## Audience/reach

- Mass-market. Once iconic American brand
- Median reader affluent (HHI $100K+) per MRI-Simmons
- 50% female/50% male
- Top 5 magazine with A18-49 adults

## Quality/reputation

- Historic gold standard for photography and exploration
- Multiple Pulitzers historically — none in recent years
- MBFC rating: Not formally rated (factual, historically high)
- 2023 layoffs raised serious quality concerns
- Remaining editorial team: freelance writers + few editors
