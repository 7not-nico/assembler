# Round 1 — Quantum Magazine

**Status:** PASS

## Editorial model

- Editorially independent online publication of Simons Foundation
- All editorial decisions by staff → E.i.C; no foundation review
- Foundation grantees receive no preferential treatment
- Professional journalists + fact-checkers on staff

## History

- Launched 2012 as Simons Science News, renamed July 2013
- Founded by Thomas Lin (ex-NYT journalist)
- E.i.C: Thomas Lin (2012–2024), Samir Patel (2024–)
- Deputy ed: Michael Moyer (ex-SciAm). Art director: Samuel Velasco

## Subject scope

- Mathematics, theoretical physics, theoretical computer science, basic life sciences
- Explicitly excludes applied science (health, medicine, engineering)

## Business model

- Nonprofit, fully funded by Simons Foundation ($5B+ endowment)
- No paywall, no ads
- Syndication partnerships: SciAm, Wired, Atlantic, WaPo
- Also operates sister pub The Transmitter (neuroscience) + Sandbox Films + Quanta Books (FSG imprint)

## Audience/reach

- Intellectually curious general readers (not specialists)
- Syndicated globally, translated into 6+ languages
- YouTube: 1M+ subscribers

## Quality/reputation

- **2022 Pulitzer Prize** in Explanatory Reporting
- National Magazine Awards: General Excellence 2020, Best Single-Topic Issue 2024
- Three Webby Awards (People's Voice)
- MBFC: Pro-Science, VERY HIGH factual reporting

## Fact-checking

- "Each article... meticulously reported, edited and fact-checked"
- Staff-written model enables systematic in-house verification
- AI Editorial Policy (2026): human oversight required, no AI drafting
