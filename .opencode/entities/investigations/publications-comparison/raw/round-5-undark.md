# Round 5 — Undark Magazine

**Status:** PASS

## Editorial model

- Nonprofit, editorially independent digital magazine
- Published under Knight Science Journalism Program at MIT
- "Not interested in science communication — but in true journalistic coverage of the sciences"
- Focus on investigative, critical science journalism — science in "both light and shadow"
- Funders have no right to review or edit content
- Full firewall between news coverage and revenue sources

## History

- Founded March 2016 in Cambridge, MA
- Co-founders: Deborah Blum (Pulitzer-winning science author) and Tom Zeller Jr. (former NYT journalist)
- Tom Zeller Jr. serves as Editor-in-Chief
- Name derived from radium-based luminous paint "Undark" — metaphor for examining science critically

## Subject scope

- Intersection of science and society
- Investigative science journalism: policy, ethics, environment, health, technology
- Long-form journalism, features, essays, op-eds, Q&As, book reviews
- Explicitly covers contentious and politically/ethically fraught aspects of science

## Business model

- Nonprofit. Primary funding: John S. and James L. Knight Foundation through KSJ@MIT
- May accept small additional gifts/grants from individuals or journalism foundations
- No anonymous donations, no government/party donations
- No paywall, free to republish (with guidelines)
- INN (Institute for Nonprofit News) member
- Donations go to editorial fund via MIT giving

## Audience/reach

- "Reaches millions of readers annually" (per KSJ@MIT)
- Syndicated to: The Atlantic, Scientific American, Smithsonian, Time, Newsweek, NPR, Quartz, Salon, Slate
- English-language only

## Quality/reputation

- George K. Polk Award 2018 — Environmental Reporting ("Breathtaking" series on global air pollution)
- Al Neuharth Innovation in Investigative Journalism Award 2019 (Online News Association)
- National Magazine Award finalist 2022 (Reporting category)
- Online Journalism Award finalist 2017 (Feature, "Wear & Tear" series)
- Anthologized in Best American Science & Nature Writing
- MBFC: Left-Center bias
