# Round 3 — The Conversation

**Status:** PASS

## Editorial model

- **Unique:** Academics write, professional journalists edit
- Authors must have proven expertise + disclose funding/affiliations
- Editors commission and shape pieces for public accessibility
- CC-licensed — free republishing for any news outlet
- AP distributes content daily to thousands of newsrooms
- No paywall, no ads

## History

- Founded 2011 (Australia) by Andrew Jaspan (ex-newspaper editor) + Jack Rejtman
- US edition launched October 2014
- 11 regional editions: Africa, Australia, Brazil, Canada, Canada (FR), Spain, Europe, France, Global, Indonesia, NZ, UK, US

## Subject scope

- All academic disciplines: policy, science, health, economics, education, history, ethics, arts
- Broadest disciplinary range of all four

## Business model

- Nonprofit (501c3 in US, charity in AU/UK)
- Revenue: university membership fees + foundation grants + reader donations
- Funders: Knight, Ford, Sloan, Moore, McGovern, Walton, Mellon, Templeton, Lumina, RWJF, HHMI, etc.
- Founding university partners: Binghamton, Tufts, Vanderbilt, UF, Ohio State, Penn State, Michigan State, U Michigan, UMass Amherst

## Audience/reach

- 28M monthly onsite page views globally
- 47M monthly including republication
- 88% of readers are non-academic
- ~40% aged 18–34
- Balanced gender split (54% M / 45% F)
- 96K+ academic authors, 188K+ articles published globally

## Quality/reputation

- INN (Institute for Nonprofit News) member
- No major journalism awards (Pulitzer etc.)
- Known for reliability, trustworthiness, evidence-based approach
- "Academic rigor, journalistic flair"
