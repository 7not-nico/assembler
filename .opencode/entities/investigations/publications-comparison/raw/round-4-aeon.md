# Round 4 — Aeon

**Status:** PASS

## Editorial model

- Long-form essays (2,500–5,000 words) + video documentaries
- Content by academics, journalists, and expert practitioners
- Accepts pitches monthly (1st–7th)
- Pay: freelance fee for professional writers, honorarium for others
- Professional editorial team
- Aeon Video: curated selections + original series (In Sight series)
- Sister site Psyche launched 2020 (psychology, philosophy, arts)
- SophiaClub launched 2022 (cultural events program)

## History

- Founded 2012 by Paul and Brigid Hains (Australian couple) in London
- Now offices in London, Melbourne, New York
- Registered as Australian charity 2016
- Registered Aeon America as 501(c)(3) in US

## Subject scope

- "Asks the biggest questions and finds the freshest, most original answers"
