# Round E — Undark Fact-Checking

**Status:** PASS

## Editorial process

- "Renowned for its rigorous and comprehensive fact-checking process" (KSJ@MIT)
- "Vigorously fact-check everything we produce"
- "Extensive editorial scrutiny" on all published material
- Internal research team handles fact-checking
- Contributors expected to fact-check against reliable and multiple sources before submitting

## AI Policy (undark.org/ai-policy)

- No AI-generated or AI-edited editorial text (proposals, drafts, published articles, newsletter, excerpts)
- Exception: when AI itself is the subject of the story
- Light copy-editing by AI permitted for non-editorial purposes (marketing, headlines, social media)
- AI as research aid (like Google Search or Wikipedia) allowed — but all original reporting must be human
- Contributors submitting AI-produced work will not be invited back

## Corrections

- Dedicated corrections policy page: https://undark.org/corrections-policy/
- "We work quickly to understand the error and to fully address it"
- Substantive corrections appended with detailed explanation at bottom of piece
- Minor changes (typos, grammar) may be made silently in hours after publication
- Report errors: corrections@undark.org
