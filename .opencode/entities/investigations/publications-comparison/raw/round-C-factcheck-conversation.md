# Round C — The Conversation Fact-Checking

**Status:** PASS

## Editorial process

- Academics write articles, professional journalists edit
- Authors must have proven expertise in their subject area
- Disclosure of funding/affiliations required (signed disclosure statement)
- Editorial charter: "fact-based and editorially independent forum, free of commercial or political bias"
- Editors reach out to leading scholars, work collaboratively on accessible writing

## Transparency

- Full funder list published on homepage and partners page
- Annual reports and IRS Form 990 publicly available
- Author disclosure statements on every article
- Editorial Charter publicly published
- "10 ways we're different" page explains the model
- Animated video explaining editorial process

## Corrections

- Dedicated corrections page: https://theconversation.com/global/corrections
- Region-specific contact for reporting errors (Africa, AU, BR, CA, ES, FR, ID, NZ, UK, US)
- Charter commitment: "Where errors occur correct them expeditiously"
- No public corrections log found — corrections likely inline per article

## Fact-checking

- No dedicated fact-checking department
- Relies on academic sourcing model: experts write from published research
- Professional editors verify for clarity, accuracy, and fairness
- Model prioritizes accessibility and trust over investigative depth
- CC-licensed free republishing enables broad distribution without paywalls

## Independent ratings

- No MBFC rating specifically for The Conversation
- INN (Institute for Nonprofit News) member
- Generally regarded as trustworthy and evidence-based
