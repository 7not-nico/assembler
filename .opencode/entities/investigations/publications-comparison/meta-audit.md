# Publications Comparison — Meta-Audit

**Topic:** Compare five publications — Quantum Magazine, National Geographic, The Conversation, Aeon, Undark Magazine

**Method:** per-publication deep dives via `/xsearch`, then synthesis

**Dimensions (Phase 1):** Editorial model, subject scope, business model, audience/reach, quality/reputation, funding source, editorial trajectory

**Dimension (Phase 2):** Fact-checking / editorial process

**Status:** Phase 1 complete, Phase 2 complete

---

## Per-Publication Summaries

### Quantum Magazine

- Founded 2012 by Simons Foundation. Renamed from Simons Science News (2013)
- Covers math, theoretical physics, CS, basic life sciences only
- Editorially independent from Simons Foundation. Staff-written, meticulously fact-checked
- Nonprofit, foundation-funded. No paywall, no ads
- Pulitzer 2022 (Explanatory Reporting), National Magazine Awards, 3 Webby Awards
- MBFC: Pro-Science, VERY HIGH factual reporting, zero failed fact-checks
- Trajectory: **Rising** — awards, ambitious series, YouTube 1M+ subs

### National Geographic

- Founded 1888 by National Geographic Society. Iconic yellow border since 1910
- Broadest scope: geography, science, history, culture, exploration, conservation
- **Major shift:** 2015 media assets sold to Fox ($725M), now 73% Disney-owned for-profit
- **June 2023:** All 19 staff writers laid off, newsstand sales ended. Work outsourced to freelancers
- Print circ: 1.8M (down from 12M peak). ~60M readers globally, 40 language editions
- Historic gold standard; multiple Pulitzers historically but **none in recent years**
- Trajectory: **Declining** — structural downsizing, quality concerns

### The Conversation

- Founded 2011 (Australia) by Andrew Jaspan. US edition 2014. 11 regional editions
- Broadest disciplinary range — all academic fields
- **Unique model:** academics write, journalists edit. CC-licensed free republishing
- Nonprofit. Funded by universities (membership fees) + foundations + reader donations
- 28M monthly onsite views, 47M including republication. 96K+ authors, 188K+ articles
- INN member. Trusted, evidence-based. No major journalism awards
- Trajectory: **Stable** — steady growth, proven model

### Aeon

- Founded 2012 by Paul & Brigid Hains (London). Offices in London, Melbourne, NY
- Scope: ideas, philosophy, culture, science, psychology, arts
- Long-form essays (2,500–5,000 words) + video documentaries
- Nonprofit charity (Aus + US 501c3). Funded by reader donations only
- Rank #189 News. 53% male, 25–34 largest age group
- MBFC: Left-Center bias, HIGH factual reporting, clean record (no failed checks in 5+ years)
- Trajectory: **Stable** — small but respected, niche authority

### Undark Magazine

- Founded March 2016 by Deborah Blum (Pulitzer-winning author) and Tom Zeller Jr. (ex-NYT), under Knight Science Journalism at MIT
- Scope: intersection of science and society — investigative journalism, policy, ethics, environment, health
- "Not interested in science communication, but in true journalistic coverage of the sciences"
- Nonprofit, INN member. Primary funding from Knight Foundation via KSJ@MIT. No paywall, CC-licensed republishing
- Syndicated to Atlantic, SciAm, Smithsonian, Time, Newsweek, NPR. "Millions of readers annually"
- George K. Polk Award 2018 (Environmental Reporting), ONA Award 2019, Nat'l Magazine finalist 2022
- MBFC: Left-Center bias. Renowned for rigorous fact-checking with dedicated internal research team
- Trajectory: **Stable** — MIT/Knight backing provides institutional stability

### Key trade-offs

- **Depth vs breadth:** Quanta (deep, narrow) >> Aeon (deep, broad) > Conversation (wide, moderate) > NatGeo (broad, thinning)
- **Independence:** Aeon (most independent) > Conversation (diversified) > Quanta (single funder, strong firewall) > NatGeo (Disney-controlled)
- **Scale:** NatGeo (mass) >> Conversation (large) > Quanta (medium) > Aeon (small)
- **Trajectory concern:** NatGeo's 2023 layoffs of all staff writers + newsstand exit signals structural decline
