# Investigation: Book Abandonment Meta-Audit

**Topic**: Cultural variations in DNF (Did Not Finish) and reading attitudes.
**Status**: Manifest Synthesized.

## 1. Foundations
- **Baseline**: `COG.VERBAL.REASONING` (reading-comprehension, critical-reasoning, text-analysis).
- **Inferred Need**: Absence of `TERM.READING.HABIT` necessitates foundational reliance on existing cognitive literacy terms.

## 2. Decomposition
- **Cognitive-Load**: Text complexity vs. reader proficiency.
- **Utility-Metric**: Collectivistic (social/communal utility) vs. Individualistic (leisure/lifestyle).
- **Opportunity-Cost**: Attention-economy; time-allocation vs. engagement.

## 3. Findings
- **Cultural Framing**: DNF as private choice (Individualistic) vs. social utility (Collectivistic).
- **Structural vs. Personal**: Academic focus on structural accessibility; Commercial focus on personal "guilt/decline."
- **Mode-Shift**: Data points to format/utility evolution rather than total reading decline.
