## Key Findings
- Russian math education tradition is world-renowned for olympiad-level problem solving and "math circles" approach
- Steklov Institute and Moscow State University are the premier research institutions
- Key texts: Shklarski-Chentzov-Yaglom, Fomin-Genkin-Itenberg "Mathematical Circles (Russian Experience)"
- IES framework on development of mathematical reasoning is cross-listed — relevant to the Russian tradition of progressive reasoning skills
- Gap: Russian tradition uses "mathematical thinking" (математическое мышление) rather than "QR" as labeled field
