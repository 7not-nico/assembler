## Key Findings
- India's quantitative reasoning ecosystem is driven by competitive exams (CAT, SSC, Bank, IIT-JEE, ISI)
- NCERT provides the official government curriculum framework for mathematics education
- ISI Kolkata and CMI Chennai are the leading academic research institutions for mathematical reasoning
- "Quantitative aptitude" is the dominant term — overlapping with but distinct from "quantitative reasoning"
- Commercial exam-prep ecosystem is vast but should be replaced with NCERT and university-hosted resources
