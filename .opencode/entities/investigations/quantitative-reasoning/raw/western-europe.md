## Key Findings
- Freudenthal Institute (Utrecht University) is the leading European institution for mathematics education — origin of Realistic Mathematics Education (RME)
- Continental Europe frames QR as "mathematical literacy" (PISA) and "mathematical reasoning" (raisonnement mathématique)
- French Ministry of Education provides detailed reasoning competence frameworks for primary/secondary
- DIPF (Germany) is the primary Leibniz institute for educational research
- VUB (Brussels) runs structured central testing aligned to Flemish curriculum
