# Pedagogy: Interdisciplinary Discourse
## Region: Germany
## Source investigation: liberal-arts-education

## Rating: PASS

## Mechanism

Studium Generale — interdisciplinary coursework combining humanities, social sciences, natural sciences, and practical skills. Students encounter multiple disciplinary methods and problem-solving strategies, fostering critical knowledge acquisition and reflection.

## Evidence

| Source | Finding | Design |
|--------|---------|--------|
| SG Netzwerk Nord (HCU Hamburg) | SG aims: deepen general education, orientation knowledge, reflection ability, interdisciplinary discourse competence (interdisziplinäre Diskursfähigkeit) | Network charter, multiple universities |
| Universität Mainz | SG as interdisciplinary science reflection; post-WWII re-founding: "broadening perspective, deepening knowledge, personal and political education" | University center, est. 1948 |
| Universität Siegen | SG structured: interdisciplinary (fachwissenschaftlich) + professional orientation (berufsorientierend); foreign languages, academic/professional competencies, IT, projects | University regulation |
| Universität Bremen (General Studies) | Profile-building through key qualifications (Schlüsselqualifikationen): methods, gender, intercultural, social competencies; self-reflection and career planning | Program documentation |
| Universität Würzburg | Humanities graduates develop: structured thinking, problem-solving, argumentation, conflict resolution, written/oral expression, creativity, self-motivation, general knowledge; valued for rapid domain entry | Program documentation |
| HRK (German Rectors' Conference) | Humboldtian Bildung: educate mature, critical, autonomous citizens; unity of Bildung (formation) and Ausbildung (training) | Expert report |

## Key Causal Pathway

```
Interdisciplinary exposure + multiple methods + practical application → Critical knowledge acquisition + reflection + integrated problem-solving
```

German model is less explicit about "critical thinking" as named outcome but builds it structurally through disciplinary boundary-crossing and method diversity.

## Gaps
- SG typically supplement to major — not full-curriculum immersion
- Empirical outcomes research less developed vs. US
- Variation across institutions — no national standard
- Recent Bologna reforms shift emphasis toward measurable competencies, potentially diluting the Humboldtian ideal
