# Pedagogy: Rhetoric & Argumentation
## Region: France
## Source investigation: liberal-arts-education + supplemental search

## Rating: PASS

## Mechanism

Systematic training in rhetorical analysis, argument construction, and critical interpretation across humanities disciplines. Core exercises: dissertation, explication de texte, essai, oral argumentation. Rooted in the French tradition of *culture générale* and *esprit critique*.

## Evidence

| Source | Finding | Design |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Programme HLP (French National Education) | 4-semester curriculum: powers of speech (rhetoric, authority, seduction); representations of the world; search for self; humanity in question. Students analyze argument structure, identify rhetorical strategies, evaluate claims | National curriculum (lycée) |
| Licence Humanités Strasbourg | 4 disciplines at equal weight (letters, history, philosophy, languages); argumentation across fields; 90-100% study abroad; research initiation with cross-disciplinary thesis | University program (30 students/yr) |
| Licence Humanités Nanterre | Unique rhetoric curriculum in France; double degree + contemporary world DU; oral expression, persuasion, presentation of self | University program |
| Université Toulouse | Key competencies: structured thinking, argumentation, dissertation, explication de textes, oral presentations, critical analysis of works | University program |
| Onisep / education.gouv.fr | HLP develops: reading, interpretation, expression, analysis of complex problems; argumentation as path to truth through proof | Official program documentation |

## Key Causal Pathway

```
Pluridisciplinary text analysis + rhetorical critique + structured argumentation (dissertation/essai) → Critical thinking + argumentation skills + autonomous judgment
```

French model is distinctive for embedding rhetoric within substantive humanities content — not teaching "critical thinking" as abstract skill but as practiced within philosophy, literature, history.

## Gaps
- Elite, limited-intake programs — not scalable to mass higher education
- Empirical outcome measurement weaker than US (no Wabash-equivalent longitudinal study)
- Rhetoric focus tied to French language/culture tradition — transferability unclear
- Emphasis on written over quantitative reasoning
