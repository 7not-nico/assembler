# Meta-Audit: TENET

**3 regions PASS**

## Etymological Chain

PIE `*ten-` "to stretch" → Latin *tenere* "to hold" → *tenet* "he holds" (3sg) → English *tenet* (early 15c)

Greek parallel: *dokeō* "to think, seem" → *dogma* "that which seems true, opinion, decree"

## Regional Summaries

### Latin — PASS
- *tenere* from PIE *ten- "to stretch"; connecting notion: stretching → maintaining → holding
- *tenet* = 3sg present indicative "he holds" — used in Medieval Latin to introduce statements of doctrine
- *tenent* = 3pl "they hold" — obsolete English variant distinguishing individual vs corporate belief
- Legal: *tenere* also "to be bound by, be subject to" (laws, contracts)
- Root metaphor: **grip/possession** — what one holds onto

### Greek — PASS
- *dogma* (δόγμα) from *dokeō* "to seem, think" = "that which seems true"
- Three senses: personal opinion, philosophical doctrine, public decree
- Sextus Empiricus: two kinds of dogma — passive assent vs active theoretical commitment
- Latin *dogma* used as equivalent of "philosophic tenet" (Cicero)
- Root metaphor: **seeming** — what appears true, less active than Latin's physical grip

### Anglophone — PASS
- Entered English early 15c from Medieval Latin doctrinal formula
- Confusion with **tenant**: both from *tenere* — tenant = "one who holds" (land), tenet = "that which is held" (belief)
- Singular *tenet* eliminated plural *tenent* by c.1725
- Modern: "a principle or belief held as absolute truth by a group"

## Comparison Table

| Region | Term | Root | Metaphor | Agency |
|--------|------|------|----------|--------|
| Latin | *tenet* | *tenere* "hold" | **Grip** — held firmly | Passive (held) |
| Greek | *dogma* | *dokeō* "seem" | **Seeming** — appears true | Passive (seems) |
| English | tenet | L *tenet* | **Possession** — maintained as true | Passive (held) |

**Unifying thread**: tenet is the only one of the four that is fundamentally **passive**. Principle is taken (active), heuristic is discovered (active), maxim is stated (active). Tenet is *held* — gripped, maintained, possessed. This is why it naturally belongs to groups and institutions: doctrines are what we *hold on to*.
