## Gaps

- **Cognitive biases**: Less coverage in Continental Europe searches compared to Anglosphere — publication bias is mentioned but confirmation bias and p-hacking less prominent
- **Source credibility frameworks**: European sources focus on evidence-level grading (GRADE) rather than source evaluation frameworks (CRAAP, SIFT)
- **Evidence synthesis innovation**: SWiM guideline (UK) not widely referenced in Continental European literature
- **Cross-language synthesis**: Each language tradition produces its own guides with minimal cross-referencing
