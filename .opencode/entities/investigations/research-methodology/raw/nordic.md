## Gaps

- **Cognitive biases research**: Limited primary research on bias detection methodology from Nordic sources (they apply existing tools rather than develop new ones)
- **Source credibility frameworks**: No Nordic equivalents of CRAAP/SIFT — they skip source evaluation in favor of GRADE evidence certainty
- **Limited Finnish-specific sources**: Most Finnish research methodology published through Swedish or international channels
- **Strong applied focus**: Nordic sources excel at operational methodology (handbooks, tools, templates) rather than theoretical meta-research
