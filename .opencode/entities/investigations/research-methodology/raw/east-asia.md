## Gaps

- **Cognitive biases**: Very limited coverage. Japan's Minds mentions publication bias in GRADE but no primary bias research
- **Source credibility frameworks**: No local equivalents of CRAAP/SIFT — Chinese/TW/JP/KR literature adopts international tools (PRISMA, AMSTAR) directly
- **Regional quality concern**: China TCM SRs (666 evaluated) and Korean journal SRs (90.5% critically low) show significant methodological quality gaps
- **Qualitative synthesis underdeveloped**: Japan's narrative synthesis section references Popay ESRG guidance but qualitative synthesis methods less developed than Nordic region
- **Distinctive contributions**: Taiwan's systematic review database (NAER), Japan's Minds GRADE-based evidence evaluation system, Korea's NECA manual — all government-backed methodology infrastructure
