## Fundamentals

Personal research methodology improvement sits at the intersection of four domains:

1. **Research workflow design** — how to structure the research process from question → search → evaluate → synthesize → apply
2. **Source quality & credibility** — how to evaluate information sources for trustworthiness, relevance, and bias
3. **Evidence synthesis methods** — how to combine findings from multiple sources systematically and transparently
4. **Cognitive biases in research** — how to detect and mitigate biases that distort findings

The dominant paradigm across all regions is **evidence-based practice (EBP)** with systematic review methodology as the gold standard. PRISMA, GRADE, and Cochrane Handbook are the most-cited frameworks globally.

## By Region

### Anglosphere (PASS — 30+ academic sources)

**Strengths**: Dominant methodology literature. Covers all 4 anchors comprehensively. Strong on bias detection (p-curve, Egger's, trim-and-fill), source evaluation (CRAAP, SIFT, RAVEN, PROMPT), and evidence synthesis (ESRC guidance, SWiM, NIRO-SR).

**Key institutions**: Oxford CEBM, Colorado State, York CRD, UGA, UMD, Michigan, USC, UCSB, LSHTM, Glasgow, Bristol, UC Davis, UC Merced, Penn.

**Key frameworks**: PICO/SPIDER/SPICE, PRISMA 2020, ESRC narrative synthesis, SWiM, GRADE, AMSTAR 2, ROBIS, RoB 2.

### Continental Europe (PASS — 32 academic sources)

**Strengths**: Strong national health technology assessment agencies (HAS/France, IQWiG/Germany). Methodologically rigorous handbooks. Each language tradition produces localized PRISMA translations and adaptation guides. Netherlands strong on practical 24-step SR guides and WEIRD population awareness.

**Key institutions**: HAS (FR), INRAE (FR), CNRS (FR), CIRAD (FR), Charité (DE), IQWiG (DE), Oldenburg (DE), Duisburg-Essen (DE), DEGAM (DE), Cochrane Deutschland, Universidad de Murcia (ES), Universidad de Sevilla (ES), Universidad de Granada (ES), Utrecht (NL), Amsterdam UMC (NL), Groningen (NL).

**Distinctive contributions**: SALSA framework (Search, Appraisal, Synthesis, Analysis); CAT method (Critically Appraised Topic); GRADE-CERQual for qualitative evidence; PI/ECOTSS for environmental epidemiology.

### Nordic (PASS — 16 academic sources)

**Strengths**: SBU (Sweden) is a world leader in evidence synthesis methodology — comprehensive 10-chapter handbook covering all study types. Strong on qualitative evidence synthesis (QES templates, SPICE framework, meta-ethnography, GRADE-CERQual). Quickstar tool for rapid SR quality assessment.

**Key institutions**: SBU (SE), Karolinska Institutet (SE), SDU (DK), Royal Danish Library (DK), UiB/CISMAC (NO), Lund University (SE), Uppsala University (SE).

**Distinctive contributions**: Quickstar rapid bias assessment; QES 7-domain quality template; 4-stage qualitative synthesis process (1st→2nd→3rd level themes); SPICE framework for qualitative questions; GRADE-CERQual adaptation.

### East Asia (PASS — 32 academic sources)

**Strengths**: Strong government-backed methodology infrastructure. China has massive SR output (666 TCM SRs evaluated in one study). Japan and Korea have comprehensive national manuals (Minds, NECA). Taiwan contributes systematic review database with effect sizes. All regions adopt and translate international standards (PRISMA, AMSTAR, GRADE, Cochrane) with native-language adaptations.

**Key institutions**: Peking Union Medical College (CN), BUCM (CN), NAER (TW), NCKU (TW), Minds/Japan Council for Quality Health Care (JP), NECA (KR), Korea Knowledge Translation.

**Distinctive contributions**: Taiwan's systematic review database with downloadable codebooks and effect sizes; Japan's Minds GRADE-based evidence body evaluation system (SR-10/SR-12 templates, SoF tables); Korea's NECA 19-chapter SR manual with ROBINS-I and GRADE integration; FAITH appraisal framework (Taiwan); Cooper's 7-step model widely cited in Japan.

**Quality concern**: Two independent studies (China TCM SRs, Korean medical journal SRs) report 90%+ of published SRs have critically low methodological quality — protocol registration and excluded study lists are the most common deficiencies.

### Cross-cutting gaps

1. **Theory-to-practice gap**: Extensive methodological literature but minimal guidance on personal/individual research skill development
2. **Tooling blind spot**: No academic literature on AI-assisted research tools (MCPs, LLM-based search) — field evolves too fast for peer-reviewed publication cycle
3. **Discipline silos**: Health dominates; social sciences, humanities, and engineering research methodology underdeveloped
4. **Cognitive bias awareness vs application**: Bias detection well-documented statistically (p-curve, funnel plots) but poorly integrated into individual researcher workflows
5. **WEIRD bias acknowledged but unresolved**: ESCA (Amsterdam) flags that most research uses WEIRD populations; solutions remain aspirational

## Recommendations for Personal Research Methodology

1. **Adopt the research pipeline** — PAT.INVESTIGATION.PIPELINE.STAGE frames research as foundations → decompose → search → synthesize. Implement each stage with tools from this survey
2. **Use PICO/SPICE for question framing** — PICO for intervention questions, SPICE for qualitative/exploratory questions
3. **Apply credibility assessment multi-level** — use CRAAP/SIFT for source triage, GRADE for evidence certainty, and Hilligoss & Rieh's 3-level framework (construct → heuristics → interaction) for overall assessment
4. **Plan synthesis method before data collection** — decide meta-analysis, narrative synthesis (ESRC/SWiM), or qualitative synthesis (ENTREQ/QES) at protocol stage
5. **Build bias detection into workflow** — p-curve for publication bias awareness, confirmation bias checklists at search/screening stages, pre-registration of protocols
6. **Prefer academic sources** — .edu / .ac.* / institutional over .com; if .com is unavoidable, flag as commercial
7. **Cross-reference regions** — methodology guidance varies by language/culture; triangulate Anglosphere with Continental European and Nordic sources for comprehensive coverage
