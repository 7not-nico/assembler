## Key Findings
- Kant's precise definition: *Maxime* = "das subjektive Prinzip des Wollens" (subjective principle of willing)
- Distinction: **Maxime** (subjective, agent-relative) vs **praktisches Gesetz** (objective, universal)
- Maxime entered German via Latin *maxima* (16th C), later influenced by French *maxime*
- Categorical imperative: "Act only according to that maxim whereby you can will that it become a universal law"
- Three levels of maxim abstraction (Kahn): i-maxims (individual tokens), a-maxims (abstract principles), k-maxims (kinds)
