## Key Findings
- **γνώμη** (gnōmē) derived from γιγνώσκω "to know" → "means of knowing" → "judgment" → "opinion" → "maxim"
- Aristotle (Rhetoric 1394a): γνώμη = a general statement about matters of action, what should be chosen/avoided
- Gnomic poets (6th C BC): Theognis, Solon — collected γνῶμαι in verse
- Latin *maxima propositio* = translation equivalent of Greek γνώμη/ἀξίωμα
- Distinction in rhetoric: γνώμη (maxim, universal statement) vs ἀπόφθεγμα (apothegm, attributed saying)
