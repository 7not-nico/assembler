## Key Findings
- **Boethius** (c. 480-524) coined *maxima propositio* in *De topicis differentiis*: the "greatest premise" that serves as warrant for inference
- The term *maxima* was substantivized from phrases like *maxima propositio*, *maxima sententia*, *maxima regula*
- In medieval logic: maximal propositions are self-evident truths underlying all topical arguments
- In law: Latin legal maxims (*pacta sunt servanda*, *de minimis non curat lex*, *rex non potest peccare*) served as foundational principles — *regulae* (rulers, measuring devices)
