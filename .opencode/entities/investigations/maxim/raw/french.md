## Key Findings
- **maxime** entered French ca. 1330 (scholastic use), moral sense ca. 1485
- La Rochefoucauld (1665): *Réflexions ou Sentences et Maximes morales* — established the literary genre
- Three terms distinguished in 17th C: *réflexion* (personal meditation), *sentence* (authoritative pronouncement), *maxime* (general truth, no author needed)
- Boethius's *maxima propositio* is the logical origin per Francis Goyet: "la maxime ne se connaît pas de supérieur"
- French moralists (La Rochefoucauld, La Bruyère, Chamfort) — maxim as *genre mineur*
- Kant's *Maxime* enters French philosophical vocabulary in 1873
