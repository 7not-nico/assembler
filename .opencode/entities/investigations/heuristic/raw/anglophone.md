## Key Findings
- English term coined 1821 — not from classical Latin but Neo-Latin scholarly coinage
- Polya (1945) "How to Solve It" — established heuristics as study of problem-solving methods
- Simon & Newell (1950s) — GPS, bounded rationality, heuristic search
- Kahneman & Tversky (1970s) — heuristics and biases (availability, representativeness, anchoring)
- Gigerenzer (1990s) — fast and frugal heuristics; ecological rationality
- Glover (1986) — coined "meta-heuristic" for high-level algorithmic frameworks
- Core definition: a method not guaranteed to be optimal but sufficient for immediate goals
