# Region: German — HEURISTIK

## Sources
- KIT (Westhauser): Gigerenzer's heuristics — Take The Best, Take The Last, Minimalist
- Academia (Einfache Heuristiken): ecological rationality; simple heuristics for complex decisions
- Academia (Heurismen): Polya's Heurismen in mathematical problem-solving
- KIT (Rudnick): Duncker — functional fixedness, insight, problem-solving Gestalt psychology
- CMU (Simon/Newell): Duncker's influence on cognitive science; Selz's schematic anticipation
- Uni Potsdam (Gigerenzer): ABC Research Group; fast & frugal heuristics program
- ZML (Waniek): Heuristics as "Kunst, mit begrenztem Wissen zu guten Lösungen zu kommen"

## Key Findings
- **Heuristik** entered German via Polya's "How to Solve It" (1945, German: "Schule des Denkens")
- Karl Duncker (1935): "Zur Psychologie des produktiven Denkens" — functional fixedness, insight
- Otto Selz: schematic anticipation as precursor to means-ends analysis
- Gigerenzer (Max Planck): simple heuristics — recognition, take-the-best, minimalist
- German tradition emphasizes **praktische Heuristik** — applied problem-solving, bounded rationality
- Core: Heuristik ≠ algorithm; it's "die Kunst, mit begrenztem Wissen zu guten Lösungen zu kommen"
