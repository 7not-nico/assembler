# Region: Greek — HEURISTIC (εὑρίσκω)

## Sources
- Perseus/LSJ: εὑρίσκω — find, discover, devise, invent, get, gain, procure
- Wiktionary: PIE *wreh₁- "to find"; cognates: OIr -fúar, OCS ob-rětъ, Arm gerem
- etymonline: heuristic (1821) irregular formation from Gk heuriskein; heureka (c.1600)
- La Roi (2020): semantic development — physical finding → mental perception → mental judgment
- Linguistics StackExchange/Beekes: root uncertain; possible *ue-urh₁-e/o- reduplicated aorist
- LSJ.gr: εὑρετικός "inventive"; εὕρημα "find, discovery"; εὕρεσις "invention"

## Key Findings
- **heuriskein** = "to find" — PIE root *wreh₁- (Beekes) or *were- (etymonline)
- Archimedes' εὕρηκα "I have found it" — the discovery exclamation
- Derivatives: εὑρετικός (heuretikos) "inventive, of a discoverer" (Plato)
- Semantic development (La Roi): Homeric physical search → Classical mental perception → mental judgment
- Core metaphor: **discovery** — not guaranteed optimal, an act of finding
