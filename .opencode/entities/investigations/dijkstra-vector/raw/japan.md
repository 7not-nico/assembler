## Key Findings
- Japanese IT glossary (e-words.jp) defines three distinct uses of "vector" in computing: 可変長配列 (variable-length array), SIMD/vector operations, and vector graphics
- The glossary does NOT mention Dijkstra in connection with any of these definitions
- Japanese Wikipedia covers Dijkstra's work extensively but does not mention the OED attribution for "vector"
- Dijkstra received the C&C Prize from NEC C&C Foundation in 2002, established by NEC — one of Japan's major tech companies

## Gaps
- No Japanese academic source attributes the term "vector" specifically to Dijkstra
- Japanese computing terminology treats "vector" as a direct import from English mathematics
- The C&C Prize citation focuses on Dijkstra's foundational software contributions (semaphores, structured programming, etc.), not specifically on terminology
