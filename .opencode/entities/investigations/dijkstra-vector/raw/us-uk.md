## Key Findings
- **Strongest evidence** is from the UT Austin Faculty Council's official "In Memoriam" resolution, which explicitly states the OED attribution for "vector" as "a sequence of consecutive locations in memory" and "stack"
- The ACM Turing Award biography (the highest honor in CS) repeats the OED attribution verbatim
- Multiple independent .edu sources confirm the same fact
- Dijkstra's specific EWD manuscript series is archived at UT Austin — the Center for American History maintains the physical originals

## Gaps
- The OED itself does not provide a free online citation; the attribution is cited *by* the UT Austin In Memoriam document and the ACM biography
- The exact electronic edition and year of the OED entry are not stated in any of the found sources
- No British university (.ac.uk) specifically analyzes EWD 41, though MacTutor (St Andrews) covers his broader contributions
