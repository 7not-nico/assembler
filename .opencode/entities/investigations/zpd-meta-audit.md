**ZPD-Metacognition gap** — ZPD and metacognition treated as parallel constructs that never converge into a unified framework across 50+ sources, 25+ countries. ZPD functions as a theoretical backdrop — invoked in introductions, never operationalized in measurement. Metacognition drives the intervention design, assessed via standard inventories (MAI, SRL microanalysis) that ignore dynamic assessment principles. Only Russia (Zaretsky multi-vector model) and France (Proust CSEN framework) build theory that bridges the two. Every region produces either ZPD research or metacognition research, never both in the same instrument.

Pattern: ZPD-intro → metacognitive-intervention → standard-assessment → no-bridge

Implication: a measurement protocol that operationalizes ZPD boundaries as a within-task metacognitive assessment variable, not a retrospective label.

Data: schemas/seed.sql — 50 sources, 6 meta-analyses, 25 researchers, 20 gaps.
