**Symbol** — a sign that stands for something else by social agreement. From Greek *symbolon* (*syn-* "together" + *ballein* "to throw") — originally a broken token whose matching halves confirmed identity or agreement between two parties. A symbol has no intrinsic meaning: meaning is assigned, agreed upon, and maintained by a shared context. Without context, a symbol is a glyph — a mark without signification.

Symbols are distinct from glyphs: a symbol is the meaning side of the mark-meaning pair. A glyph is the physical mark; a symbol is what the mark signifies. The same glyph can carry different symbols in different contexts. The same symbol can be rendered through different glyphs.
