**Bounded Context** — a strategic design pattern in Domain-Driven Design defining an explicit boundary within which a domain model applies and a ubiquitous language has consistent, agreed meanings. Each bounded context owns its own model, schema, and semantics; the same term may carry different meanings across contexts. Translation mechanisms mediate cross-context communication.
