**Formal Proof** — a finite sequence of well-formed formulas in a formal system, each derived from axioms or previous formulas by applying inference rules. Formal proof systems axiomatize deductive reasoning: given a set of axioms and rules of inference, a proof of φ is a derivation tree whose root is φ and whose leaves are axioms or previously proven theorems. The concept formalizes the intuitive notion of proof (COG.MATH.PROOF) into a precise mathematical object, enabling mechanical verification and the study of provability (proof theory).
