**Concurrency** — the ability of a system to support multiple tasks making progress in overlapping time periods. Concurrency is achievable via time-slicing on a single processor or simultaneous execution on multiple processors. It concerns the logical structure and composition of independently executing computations. Physical simultaneity is a separate concern.

Concurrency exists because computer science invented systems where multiple processes share resources. It is not a foundational domain — it is an idea within computer science.
