**Number** — The discovered form of quantity. From Latin *numerus* "a number," from PIE root *nem-* "to assign, allot, take" — the same root gives *nemesis* (distribution of what is due). A number is not invented but discovered as a property of the world: counting pebbles, measuring grain, dividing land.

The concept of number is the oldest mathematical abstraction. Every human culture discovered number independently. The earliest known representations are tally marks on bone (Ishango bone, ~20,000 BCE). Numbers exist whether or not any organism counts them — three rocks are three rocks regardless of a counter. Number is thus a discovered form: the world presents discrete quantity before humans name it.
