**Subject-Object-Verb (SOV)** — a linguistic word-order typology where clauses follow Subject → Object → Verb sequence (e.g., Japanese, Korean, Turkish). Distinct from English's SVO default and from the AMANDA lambda-linguistics convention (`subject.verb`), which uses a compressed SV form. SOV places the verb last, after both agent and target — relevant to understanding sentence parsing in code-as-language systems and language typology generally.
