**Rational Number** — The discovered form of ratio. From Latin *ratio* "reckoning, calculation, reason" — a number expressible as the quotient p/q of two integers p and q (q ≠ 0). The integers embed into the rationals as p/1. Rationals are the minimal set closed under division (excluding zero).

Rational numbers are discovered wherever partition occurs: dividing a field, sharing grain, measuring length in fractional units. Every rational has a decimal representation that either terminates (1/2 = 0.5) or repeats (1/3 = 0.333...). The Pythagoreans believed all numbers were rational — until the discovery of √2 as incommensurable shattered that assumption and revealed the irrational.
