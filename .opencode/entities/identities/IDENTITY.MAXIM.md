Each maxim declares its principle in a `principle:` field, derives corollaries from its categorization section (uses line-junction notation: emphasis, exception, qualification, precedence), and omits concrete examples. Maxims are orthogonal — no `precedes` between maxims. Source is always external (INSP.*, published works, attributed originators).
