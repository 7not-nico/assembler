**Organelle** — a specialized subcellular structure within a living cell that performs a distinct function, typically enclosed within its own lipid bilayer membrane. Organelles compartmentalize incompatible biochemical processes within the same cell, enabling eukaryotic complexity that prokaryotes lack. "Little organs" — the cell's functional subunits.
