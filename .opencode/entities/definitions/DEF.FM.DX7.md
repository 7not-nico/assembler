**DX7** — 6-operator digital FM synthesizer, 32 fixed algorithms, 16-voice polyphonic. Architecture: linear FM per Chowning-Yamaha patent, sine-wave operators configurable as carrier or modulator per algorithm. Operators grouped by algorithm topology (stack, branch, root, carrier-only). Custom YM2128 (OPS) operator chip and YM2129 (EGS) envelope generator chip. 61-key velocity/aftertouch keyboard. MIDI in/out/thru. No analog filters, no onboard effects. Patch storage via internal battery-backed RAM and ROM cartridge.
