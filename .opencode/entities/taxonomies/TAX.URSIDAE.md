**Ursidae** — a family of large-bodied mammals commonly known as bears, characterized by plantigrade locomotion, massive skulls, short tails, and a keen sense of smell. From Latin *ursus* "bear." Ursids are found across North America, Europe, Asia, and South America (Andean bear). The family includes eight extant species across three subfamilies.
