**Carnivora** — an order of mammals specialized for eating meat, characterized by sharp canine teeth and carnassial molars. From Latin *caro* "flesh" + *vorare* "to devour." Carnivorans range from the polar bear (largest land carnivore) to the least weasel. The order includes 16 extant families divided into Feliformia (cat-like) and Caniformia (dog-like).
