**Mammalia** — a class of warm-blooded, air-breathing vertebrates characterized by mammary glands, hair or fur, and a neocortex. From Latin *mamma* "breast." Mammals are distinguished by endothermy, three middle ear bones, and live birth in most species (except monotremes). The class includes over 5,400 species across 26 orders.
