**Grizzly Bear** — a large subspecies of brown bear (*Ursus arctos horribilis*) native to western North America, distinguished by its humped shoulders, dish-shaped face, and long curved claws. Grizzlies inhabit forests, alpine meadows, and coastal regions from Alaska through western Canada and into the northwestern United States. Omnivorous diet varies seasonally: salmon runs, berries, roots, and small mammals.
