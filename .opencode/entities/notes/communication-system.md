The 10 Principles of the Communication System
Sentence Structure: Every sentence follows a standard subject-first structure 
(Subject → Verb → Object).

Active Voice: Use the active voice instead of passive voice.
Affirmative Tone: Phrase rules positively (state what to do rather than what not to do).
Primary Actor First: Place the main agent or subject at the very beginning of the sentence.
Direct Action Verbs: Drive sentences with strong, direct action verbs.
Simple Base Nouns: Use simple root nouns rather than complex nominalizations (words ending in -tion, -ment, or -ity).
Finite Verbs: Use clear, conjugated verbs and avoid infinitives or gerunds (-ing words).
Explicit Function Words: Retain function words like the, a, and, and prepositions for clarity.
Declarative Statements: Express ideas as factual, declarative statements rather than commands or imperatives.
Concise Expression: Keep speech concise, precise, and focused on core facts.
