**Pattern vs Term** — use protocols to contract *rules* (protocol, enforcement, status), patterns to prescribe *how* (principle, enforcement, rules), nexi to chain patterns into *compositions* (nexus), illustrations to walk through *instances* (illustration, illustrates), and terms to define *what* (description, reference, related concepts). Sync all to `patlib.db` via `write-sync`.

Scope: entity-level. Classify .opencode source files.

Heuristic — check for `protocol:` to identify a protocol; `principle:` to identify a pattern; `nexus:` or `composition:` to identify a nexus; `illustration:` to identify an illustration; `reference:` to identify a term.

Composes with `RUL.SYSTEM.CLASSIFICATION` — one of 2 entity classification rules.
