**Lambda-linguistics** — sentences and thoughts are `subject.object.verb` instead of full phrases.

**Fallback** — single-action references. Multi-step chains use arrows: `db.query → file.write → icon.replace`.

**Compat** — lambda and pseudo-code notation compose rather than conflict: lambda names the action (`subject.action`), pseudo-code wires the flow (`→`, `::`). Use dots for agent+verb, arrows for sequence, `::` for type.
