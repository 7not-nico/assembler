**Chess** — a two-player strategy board game played on an 8×8 grid. Its pieces — king, queen, rooks, bishops, knights, pawns — each move according to fixed rules, with the objective of checkmating the opponent's king. Serves as a canonical model of perfect-information, zero-sum, turn-based combinatorial game theory. Its depth and complexity — more possible games than atoms in the observable universe — make it a benchmark for artificial intelligence and computational strategy.
