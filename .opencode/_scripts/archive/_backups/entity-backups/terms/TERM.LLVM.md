**LLVM** — a collection of modular and reusable compiler and toolchain technologies. Originated as a research project at the University of Illinois with the goal of providing a modern, SSA-based compilation strategy supporting both static and dynamic compilation of arbitrary programming languages. Its name is not an acronym — it is the full name of the project. Includes subprojects such as **Clang** (C/C++/Objective-C frontend), **MLIR** (multilevel intermediate representation), **libc++** (C++ standard library), and **LLD** (linker). Serves as the compilation backend for languages including C, C++, Rust, Swift, Julia, and Zig. Licensed under Apache 2.0 with LLVM exceptions.
