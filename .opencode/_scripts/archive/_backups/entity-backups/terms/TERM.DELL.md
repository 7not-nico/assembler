**Dell Inc.** — an American computer technology company headquartered in **Round Rock, Texas**. Founded on May 3, 1984 by **Michael Dell** as **PC's Limited** — a dormitory-room startup selling IBM PC-compatible computers built from stock parts. Renamed Dell Computer Corporation in 1987 and later Dell Inc. in 2003. Invented the **direct-to-consumer** sales model: build-to-order PCs sold without retail intermediaries, enabling aggressive pricing and efficient supply-chain management. Product lines include consumer PCs (**Inspiron**, **XPS**, **Alienware**), business workstations (**Precision**, **OptiPlex**, **Latitude**), enterprise servers (**PowerEdge**), and storage (**PowerVault**, **Dell EMC**). Landmark acquisitions: **Alienware** (2006), **Perot Systems** (2009, entering IT services), and **EMC Corporation** (2016, forming **Dell Technologies**). Traded on Nasdaq and later NYSE. Revenue of $88.4 billion in fiscal 2024 with approximately 120,000 employees worldwide.
