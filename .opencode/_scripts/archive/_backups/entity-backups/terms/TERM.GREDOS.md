**Gredos Collection** — Spanish editorial project inaugurated in 1977 by **Editorial Gredos**, comprising authoritative Spanish translations of ancient Greek and Latin classical works. Formally known as **Biblioteca Clásica Gredos**, it features dark blue hardcover binding with gold lettering. Translations are based on the most accredited critical editions of the originals, accompanied by introductions, explanatory notes, and indices — each volume reviewed by a specialist reviser. Encompasses 415 published titles (as of 2017) spanning epic, tragedy, comedy, history, philosophy, poetry, and rhetoric. Coordinated by Carlos García Gual (Greek section) and José Javier Iso and José Luis Moralejo (Latin section). Acquired by Grupo RBA in 2006; a revised 150-title reissue launched in 2019 as **Nueva Biblioteca Clásica Gredos**.
