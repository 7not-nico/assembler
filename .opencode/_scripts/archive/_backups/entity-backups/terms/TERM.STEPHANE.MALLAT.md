**Stéphane Mallat** — French mathematician and computer scientist, Professor at the **Collège de France** holding the Chair of Data Science, and member of the **CNRS** Computer Science Department at **École Normale Supérieure**. Born 1962; graduate of **École Polytechnique**, PhD from the **University of Pennsylvania** (1988). Introduced **multiresolution theory** for constructing wavelet orthonormal bases and the **fast wavelet transform** (Mallat algorithm) — foundational to the **JPEG-2000** image compression standard. Originated **matching pursuit** for sparse signal representation and the **scattering transform** for invariant object recognition. Author of *A Wavelet Tour of Signal Processing: The Sparse Way*. Recipient of the **CNRS Gold Medal** (2025). Member of the US National Academy of Sciences, National Academy of Engineering, and Academy of Technology.
