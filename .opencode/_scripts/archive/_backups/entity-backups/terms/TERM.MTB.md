**MTB Bike** (Mountain Bike) — a bicycle designed specifically for off-road cycling over rough terrain. Originated in 1970s California, pioneered by riders such as Tom Ritchey, Gary Fisher, and Keith Bontrager. Key features distinguish it from road bicycles: suspension fork (front) and often a rear suspension mechanism, large knobby tires, durable wheels, powerful disc brakes, straight wide handlebars, and wide-ratio gearing — now predominantly **1x drivetrains** (single front chainring with wide-range rear cassette). Subtypes include **cross-country** (XC), **trail**, **all-mountain**, **enduro**, **freeride**, and **downhill** (DH) — each optimized for different terrain and riding style. Governed competitively by the **Union Cycliste Internationale** (UCI) for World Cup and World Championship events. Frame materials range from aluminum and carbon fiber to steel and titanium.
