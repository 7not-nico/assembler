**Logitech** — a Swiss manufacturer of computer peripherals, headquartered in **Lausanne, Switzerland** with additional operations in **Silicon Valley**. Founded in 1981 in **Apples, Switzerland**, by **Daniel Borel**, **Pierluigi Zappacosta**, and **Giacomo Marini**. The name blends *logi* (from French "logiciel" — software) and *tech* (technology). Initially developed desktop publishing software for **Ricoh**, then prototyped its first computer mouse (the **P4**) in 1982 at Comdex. Achieved OEM agreements with **Apple** (1987), **IBM** (1989), and established the **MouseMan** ergonomic family (1991). Expanded into video communications with the **QuickCam** webcam acquisition (1998), audio with speakers and headsets, and gaming peripherals. Publicly traded on the **SWX Swiss Exchange** (LOGN) and **NASDAQ** (LOGI), added to the NASDAQ-100 in 2007. Annual revenues exceeding $1.8 billion. Product lines include control devices (mice, keyboards, trackballs), video communications (webcams), gaming controllers, and audio (speakers, headsets).
