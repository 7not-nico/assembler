**Christianity** — an Abrahamic monotheistic religion based on the life, teachings, death, and resurrection of **Jesus of Nazareth** (the **Christ**, or Anointed One of God). Originating as a movement of 1st-century Jews in the Roman province of Judea who proclaimed Jesus as the promised messiah and incarnate Son of God. Its sacred scripture is the **Bible** — comprising the **Old Testament** (shared with Judaism) and the **New Testament** (Gospels, Epistles, and Revelation). Central tenets include the **Incarnation** of God in Jesus, his atoning crucifixion, bodily resurrection, and the **Trinity** (Father, Son, and Holy Spirit). Shaped by the missionary work of **Saint Paul**, legalized under **Emperor Constantine I** (Edict of Milan, AD 313), and established as the Roman state religion under **Theodosius I** (AD 380). Major divisions: **Roman Catholicism**, **Eastern Orthodoxy**, **Oriental Orthodoxy**, and **Protestantism** (emerging from the 16th-century Reformation). With approximately 2.4 billion adherents (31% of the global population), Christianity is the world's largest religion, present on all seven continents.
