**LLM (Large Language Model)** — a deep neural network trained on vast text corpora to understand and generate human language. Its architecture relies on **tokens** and **context windows** to process and produce text. The **transformer** mechanism drives its ability to weigh relationships across all tokens simultaneously. LLMs power **RAG** pipelines by grounding generation in retrieved external knowledge. They reduce hallucination and enable live data access without retraining. Through **MCP** protocols, LLMs interact with tools and data sources dynamically. **Fine-tuning** adapts a base model to specialized domains and tasks. **Prompt engineering** shapes output through instruction design and formatting. **Quantization** compresses models for deployment on consumer hardware and edge devices. These models serve as the foundation of modern AI assistants, chatbots, and code generation systems. They extend to translation, summarization, reasoning, and enterprise search across domains. Ongoing research targets reasoning depth, factual reliability, and multi-modal perception.
