**Hello Kitty** — a fictional character created by **Yuko Shimizu** in 1974 and owned by the Japanese company **Sanrio**. She is depicted as a white anthropomorphic cat with a red bow and no visible mouth — a design choice that invites emotional projection rather than prescribing a mood. Hello Kitty is a global icon of kawaii (cute) culture and one of the most commercially successful media franchises in history.
