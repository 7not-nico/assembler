**Super Mario World** — a 1990 platform game developed and published by **Nintendo** for the **Super Nintendo Entertainment System** (SNES). Released in Japan on November 21, 1990 as a launch title for the Super Famicom; in North America on August 23, 1991; and in Europe in April 1992. Directed by **Takashi Tezuka** and produced by **Shigeru Miyamoto**, with music by **Koji Kondo**. Introduced **Yoshi**, Mario's dinosaur companion, who can swallow enemies and gain abilities from colored shells. Replaced the Super Leaf (from _Super Mario Bros. 3_) with the **Cape Feather**, enabling flight and gliding mechanics. Features a nonlinear world map with 96 exits across seven main worlds and numerous secret areas, including **Star Road** and the **Special Zone**. Built on a 4-Mbit cartridge with enhanced Mode 7 graphics, multi-layered scrolling, and expanded color palette. One of the best-selling SNES titles at over 20 million copies worldwide. Often cited as one of the greatest video games of all time.
