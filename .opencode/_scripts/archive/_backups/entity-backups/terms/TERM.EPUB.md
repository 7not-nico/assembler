**EPUB** — a free and open distribution and interchange format standard for digital publications and documents, maintained by the **International Digital Publishing Forum** (IDPF, now part of the **World Wide Web Consortium**). Defines a means of representing, packaging, and encoding structured Web content — HTML, CSS, SVG, images, and other resources — in a single-file ZIP-based container with the `.epub` extension. Current major version is EPUB 3, harmonized as **ISO/IEC TS 30135**. Supports reflowable and fixed-layout content, rich media, interactivity, global typography, and text-audio synchronization via Media Overlays. The three core sub-specifications are: **EPUB Package** (metadata, manifest, spine), **EPUB Content Documents** (XHTML, SVG, CSS profiles), and **EPUB Open Container Format** (OCF — ZIP packaging model).
