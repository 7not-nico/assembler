**Printer** — an external hardware output device that produces a durable representation of graphics or text on physical media, typically paper. Receives electronic data from a computer or other device and converts it into a hard copy through one of several technologies: **inkjet** (propels microscopic ink droplets through nozzles), **laser** (uses a laser beam and electrostatic drum to transfer toner, fused by heat), or **impact** (strikes an ink ribbon with pins, as in dot matrix printers). Connects via USB, Ethernet, or Wi-Fi. Considered a peripheral — not required for computer operation, but essential for physical document output.
