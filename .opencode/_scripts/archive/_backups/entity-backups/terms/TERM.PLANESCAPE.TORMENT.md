**Planescape: Torment** — a 1999 isometric role-playing game by Black Isle Studios, set in the Planescape campaign setting of Dungeons & Dragons. Its narrative: an immortal amnesiac known as the Nameless One wakes on a mortuary slab and must uncover his past across multiple planes of existence. Distinguished by its emphasis on dialogue, philosophy, and moral choice over combat — widely regarded for its literary depth, with over a million words of script. Its central question — "What can change the nature of a man?" — drives every interaction and outcome.
