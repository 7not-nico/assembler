**EPUBLibre** — a Spanish-language digital community platform offering free, direct-download ebooks in EPUB, PDF, and MOBI formats. Operates under the motto "más libros, más libres" (more books, more free). Its catalog, maintained by community volunteers, spans tens of thousands of titles — including public-domain classics, translated works, and crowd-corrected editions with professional-level layout. Registration is free and requires no payment data. The original platform ceased operation and subsequently re-emerged at epublibre.gratis. Its philosophy centers on free and universal access to written culture in Spanish.
