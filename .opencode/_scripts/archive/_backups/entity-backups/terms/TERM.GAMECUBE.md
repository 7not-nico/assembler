**GameCube** — a sixth-generation home video game console developed and marketed by **Nintendo**. Released in Japan on September 14, 2001; in North America on November 18, 2001; and in Europe on May 3, 2002. Codenamed **Dolphin** during development. Powered by a custom **IBM PowerPC "Gekko"** CPU at 485 MHz (with 50 additional SIMD instructions for 3D graphics) and an **ATI/Nintendo "Flipper"** GPU at 162 MHz with 3 MB of embedded 1T-SRAM (2 MB framebuffer/Z-buffer + 1 MB texture cache). System memory: 24 MB MoSys 1T-SRAM main RAM plus 16 MB DRAM auxiliary. Used proprietary 8 cm **GameCube Game Disc** (1.5 GB capacity, based on Matsushita optical disc technology). Audio: 64-channel ADPCM via a Macronix 16-bit DSP at 81 MHz. Introduced the **WaveBird** wireless controller and supported four controller ports, two memory card slots, and progressive scan output (480p). Sold approximately 21.7 million units worldwide. Backward-compatible with **Game Boy Advance** via the Game Boy Player accessory.
