**Antipattern** — a commonly used but counterproductive response to a recurring problem. Its defining quality: appears to be a valid solution but produces negative consequences that outweigh the benefits. Distinct from a mere mistake or bug — an antipattern is a documented, repeated practice that arises from misapplied patterns, misunderstood trade-offs, or short-term thinking. Cataloging antipatterns is the mirror of cataloging patterns: knowing what not to do is as valuable as knowing what to do.
