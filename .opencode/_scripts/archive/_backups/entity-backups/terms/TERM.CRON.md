**cron** — a time-based job scheduler in Unix-like operating systems. Its mechanism: a daemon (crond) reads configuration files (crontabs) and executes commands at specified intervals — minutes, hours, days of month, months, days of week. The cron expression format — five fields plus a command — serves as the de facto standard for scheduling in Linux, BSD, and macOS. Named after the Greek chronos (time).
