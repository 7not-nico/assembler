**Machine Learning** — the science and engineering of building systems that improve their performance on tasks through experience. Extracts patterns, models, and decision rules from data — without being explicitly programmed for every case. Serves as the primary driver of modern artificial intelligence: most AI capabilities today are delivered through machine learning. Its formal framing: a program learns from experience E with respect to task T and performance measure P if its performance at T, as measured by P, improves with E.
