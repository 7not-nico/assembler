**Gran Área Metropolitana** (**GAM**) — the largest urban agglomeration in Costa Rica, encompassing the conurbation of the four principal cities — **San José**, **Alajuela**, **Cartago**, and **Heredia** — all located in the **Central Valley** (Meseta Central). The GAM covers approximately 2,044 km² (4% of national territory) and holds roughly 3.3 million inhabitants — about 60% of Costa Rica's population. It is the most urbanized, densely populated, and economically active region of the country, serving as the focal point for government institutions, services, infrastructure, and commerce. The metropolitan area spans 31 cantons and 184 districts, with its urban planning governed by the **Plan GAM 2013–2030**, a binding regional development framework that succeeded the original 1982 plan mandated by Costa Rica's **Urban Planning Law No. 4240** (1968). The GAM's boundaries are defined by the **Central Volcanic Cordillera** to the north, the **Aserrí mountains** to the south, **Paraíso canton** (Cartago) to the east, and **Atenas canton** (Alajuela) to the west.
