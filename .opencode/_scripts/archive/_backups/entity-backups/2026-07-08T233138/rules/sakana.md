**Sakana Principles**

**Own direction** — choose what matters. The red fish swims against the school.

**Understanding over implementation** — every note, every response demonstrates comprehension, not completion. Can you explain why?

**Depth over breadth** — one unconventional idea well-founded beats five standard tweaks. Few sources studied deeply > many skimmed.

**Biggest risk first** — build minimum to test riskiest hypothesis. Attack the hardest concept before the easy ones.

**Small team excellence** — focused scope with few well-chosen elements beats an exhaustive catalog. Four deep sources > thirty abandoned.

**Open by default** — work in public, share findings, default to transparent.

**Own context, global standard** — personal framing with universal rigor. Rigorous within personal framing.
