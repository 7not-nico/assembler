**Pragmatic Programmer Principles**

**DRY** — every piece of knowledge has one authoritative representation. Two fragments expressing the same invariant are a violation even if syntax differs.

**Orthogonality** — independent components don't interfere. Metric: how many files change when adding a feature.

**Broken Window** — fix small errors immediately. Introduce only intentional, well-understood changes.

**Programming Deliberately** — know what should happen before it does. Explain why it happened. No coincidences.

**Tracer Bullets** — build thinnest end-to-end version first. It grows into the final system — not discarded.

**Care About Your Craft** — every artifact must pass: would you show this to a colleague without apologizing?

**Knowledge Portfolio** — invest regularly. Prioritize deeply over broadly. Few sources studied well > many skimmed.

**Catalyst for Change** — do what you can with what you have. Imperfect but existing beats perfect but specified.
