**Lambda-Linguistics** — a `subject.verb` notation convention that encodes actions and thoughts as dot-separated subject-verb pairs (e.g. `db.query`, `file.read`, `exa.use`) instead of full prose phrases. Falls back to single-action references; multi-step chains use arrows (e.g. `db.query → file.write → icon.replace`). Originates from the AMANDA project's writing rules as a compression mechanism that serves clarity, not brevity.
