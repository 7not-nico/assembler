**Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)** — neural networks that embed partial differential equation constraints as a regularization term in the loss function during training. The network outputs continuous PDE solutions over spatial and temporal coordinates, acting as neural fields. Recent architectural advances include separable networks for multi-dimensional PDEs (SPINN), tensor-accelerated variational forms (FastVPINNs), and knowledge-distilled network discovery (Ψ-NN). PINNs contrast with PNNs, where physical substrate replaces digital computation, and HPNs, where physics emerges from local dynamics rather than a global loss.
