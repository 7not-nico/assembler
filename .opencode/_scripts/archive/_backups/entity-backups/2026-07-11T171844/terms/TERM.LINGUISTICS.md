**Linguistics** — the scientific study of language. Its focus is the systematic investigation of the properties of particular languages as well as the characteristics of language in general. Core subfields include phonetics (speech sounds), phonology (sound patterns), morphology (word structure), syntax (sentence structure), semantics (meaning), pragmatics (contextual use), historical linguistics (language change), and sociolinguistics (language and society). Cross-cuts with anthropology, philosophy, psychology, computer science, and education.
