**Abstract** — a concise, self-contained summary of a research paper, thesis, or scholarly work that enables readers to quickly assess its relevance without reading the full text. An abstract communicates purpose, methods, key findings, and conclusions in a standalone format while excluding citations, figures, background exposition, and content absent from the main text. It serves two roles: reader selection (deciding whether to read the full paper) and database indexing (enabling discoverability through keyword search).
