**Term Naming Convention** — term IDs follow a hierarchical dot-separated pattern with uppercase segments. Broad domains use a single segment; sub-domains use `{DOMAIN}.{SPECIFIC}`. Shared prefixes group related terms under a common domain. The title is human-readable capitalised text, not derived from the ID. The `source` field is `assembler` for project-specific terms or `general` for cross-domain concepts. Apologias use the `APO.*` prefix for philosophical boundary documents that defend the boundaries of the type system.
