**Accounting** — the systematic process of identifying, measuring, recording, classifying, summarizing, and communicating financial information about economic entities. Originates in ancient Mesopotamia and Egypt (~4000 BCE) with clay-tablet transaction records. Modern double-entry bookkeeping was first codified by **Luca Pacioli** in his 1494 _Summa de Arithmetica_, building on earlier descriptions by **Benedetto Cotrugli** (1458). Core branches include **financial accounting** (external reporting under GAAP/IFRS), **management accounting** (internal decision-support), **tax accounting**, **cost accounting**, and **project accounting**. Governed by standard-setting bodies: **Financial Accounting Standards Board (FASB)** for US GAAP and **International Accounting Standards Board (IASB)** for IFRS. Underpins the CR-news-outlets project's price index methodology and financial reporting analysis.
