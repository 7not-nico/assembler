**Prepend** — opposite of append; adds an element to the beginning of a list or array. While `append` inserts at index `length`, `prepend` inserts at index `0`. Languages variously implement this as `unshift` (JavaScript), `insert(0, ...)` (Python), or `push_front` (C++).
