**Abstract Omission** — excludes non-representative content from research abstracts.

## Rules

- **No citations** — abstract summarizes your work, not others'
- **No figures or tables** — nor references to them
- **No absent content** — every claim must appear in the full paper
- **No lengthy background** — max 2 sentences; shortest section
- **No hooks or quotes** — no attention-grabbing devices
- **No undefined acronyms** — abstract must stand alone
- **No jargon** — must be intelligible without the full text
- **No exaggeration** — conclusions must match data
- **No meta-description** — avoid "this paper will discuss..."
- **No definitions** — reader knows the field
- **No self-evaluation** — report, do not appraise
- **No examples** — illustrate in the paper, not the abstract

## Applicability

Research papers, theses, conference submissions, grant proposals — any scholarly abstract, regardless of academic tradition.
