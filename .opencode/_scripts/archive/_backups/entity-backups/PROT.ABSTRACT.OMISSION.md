Includes only representative content in research abstracts.

## Rules

- **Summarize your work, not others'** — citations belong in the full paper body
- **Describe with text only** — figures and tables belong in the full paper; reference them by number when needed
- **Include only present content** — every claim must appear in the full paper
- **Keep background short** — max 2 sentences; shortest section
- **Open with direct substance** — state findings and conclusions, not framing or hooks
- **Define all acronyms** — abstract must stand alone
- **Use plain language** — must be intelligible without the full text
- **Match conclusions to data** — claims must match evidence
- **Describe the work directly** — state what the paper does, found, or argues
- **Assume field knowledge** — reader knows the domain
- **Report what the paper establishes** — describe methods and findings, not evaluations
- **Illustrate in paper** — examples belong in the full text

## Applicability

Research papers, theses, conference submissions, grant proposals — any scholarly abstract, regardless of academic tradition.
