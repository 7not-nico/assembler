**Left-edge deletion** — drops articles and possessive determiners at clause start.

Articles — `a`, `an`, `the`

Possessive determiners — `my`, `your`, `his`, `her`, `its`, `our`, `their`

Example: `Agent returns null` not `The agent returns null`

Distinct from dash-pivot: dash-pivot drops at emphasis pivots mid-line; this is clause-initial only.
