**Expletive deletion** — drops dummy subjects in expletive constructions.

Expletive forms — `there is`, `there are`, `there was`, `there were`, `it is`, `it was`, `it will be`

Example: `Three tools exist` not `There are three tools`

Distinct from zero copula: copula is about the verb; expletives are about dummy subjects. An expletive clause keeps its copula after the dummy is dropped.
