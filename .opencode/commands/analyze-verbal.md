Apply verbal-reasoning analysis to `$ARGUMENTS`

1. Survey the text — identify type (narrative/argumentative/expository), structural markers, purpose, scope, and author
2. Extract literal content — identify main ideas, supporting claims, factual assertions, and key terms. Distinguish major from minor points. Create a proposition inventory
3. Map structure — trace relationships: cause-effect, contrast, sequence, generalization-example. Note transitions and organizational pattern
4. Draw inferences — connect stated information to derive implicit conclusions. Use → for inference chains. Flag beyond-text claims as speculative
5. Evaluate critically — assess argument strength, evidence quality, author perspective, and bias. Distinguish fact from opinion
6. Synthesize — produce claim + evidence + reasoning chain. Anchor conclusions to specific textual evidence
