Dummy subjects drop in expletive constructions.

Scope: sentence-level. Dummy subjects drop.

Expletive forms — `there is`, `there are`, `there was`, `there were`, `it is`, `it was`, `it will be`

Example: `Three tools exist` not `There are three tools`

Distinct from zero copula: copula describes the verb; expletives describe dummy subjects. Keep expletive clause copula after the dummy drops.

Composes with `RUL.WRITING.CONVENTION` — one of 22 writing conventions.
