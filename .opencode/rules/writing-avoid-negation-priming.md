The target state names the rule; "X: disabled" or "X: excluded" carry the state in place of "do not use X" or "never do Y".

Scope: clause-level. Apply to every negative directive in LLM-facing instructions.

Composes with `RUL.WRITING.CONVENTION` — one of 22 writing conventions.
