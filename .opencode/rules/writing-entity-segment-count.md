Axiomatic and Composition group entities (MAX, SPEC, PROT, PAT, NEX, ILL, REF) use 4-segment IDs: `PREFIX.DOMAIN.SUBJECT.ASPECT`. Encyclopedic, Architectonic, and Chronicle entities use 3-segment IDs: `PREFIX.DOMAIN.SUBJECT`. IDENTITY.* is the exception at 2 segments.

Scope: entity-creation, entity-rename, audit tasks. Applies before creating or renaming any patlib entity.

Composes with `RUL.WRITING.CONVENTION` — one of 22 writing conventions.

Composes with `SPEC.ENTITY.SEGMENT.COUNT` — segment count per ring group specification; `SPEC.ENTITY.DISCERNIBILITY.SEGMENT` — segment semantics and Latin names.
