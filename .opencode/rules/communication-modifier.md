Modifiers, qualifiers, and adverbial content attach after the core statement using ordinal markers to express precedence. The ordinal sequence `-x` precedes `-y`, `-y` precedes `-z`.

Scope: sentence-level. Applies after the core SOV/SV statement.

```
subject sleep
-x green
-y colorless
-z furious
```

This separates the core proposition from its qualifiers while preserving ordinal precedence.

Composes with `RUL.COMMUNICATION.SYSTEM` — one of 12 communication principles.
